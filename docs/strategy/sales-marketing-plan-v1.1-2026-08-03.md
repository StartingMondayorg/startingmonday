# Sales & Marketing Plan — Starting Monday + MandateSignal

## 0. Where we actually are (honest baseline)

- Revenue: $0. Checks written: 0. This is the only number that matters yet.
- Assets: 25 SM pilot users · 6 trials/demos sent · ~17 warm conversations
  (largely = the informant corpus) · Alexei referral partnership at 20% ·
  working outbound infrastructure (HubSpot/Apollo/3 mailboxes, deliverability
  85/100 and climbing) · a validated ICP (boutique/transition-focused, not
  generic coaches) · a proof asset no competitor has (the brief).
- Motion learned from 16 interviews: timing opens doors, relationships close;
  proof-first; per-mandate pricing; mid-market ICP; word-on-the-street
  framing; the message-that-IS-a-lead outperforms any pitch.
- Founder constraint: Rich closes and is the face; everything mechanical
  gets automated (Sol) or outsourced (Mo/VA) when it earns its cost.

## 1. Strategy: two products, two motions

**MandateSignal (revenue priority): founder-led, lead-led, low-volume.**
~100 customers at $1,000/mandate/month = $100K MRR target. A few hundred
boutiques per niche; niches don't refill when burned. This motion is
precision, not volume: trigger-gated N1 messages (the lead IS the message),
Rich sends, Rich closes, $250 pilot converts to per-mandate
subscription (ratified ladder, 2026-08-03; amended same day: $250 pilot
supersedes the $750 sample, one-time credit on conversion).
Agencies and SDRs are structurally wrong here: they can't carry Rich's
credibility, can't hold a research-frame conversation with a search
professional, and can't fake possession of a real lead.

**Starting Monday (channel + volume): outsourced-mechanical, product-led.**
Candidates and coaches are numerous; conversations optional (Rich's own
words to Curtis). This is where sequences, volume email, and Mo's machine
belong: coach-channel nurture, signal-triggered executive outreach using
SM's own briefing data, execs-in-transition lists. Product-led trial does
the closing where possible.

**The flywheel connecting them:** every MandateSignal brief and every SM
briefing is both delivery AND marketing ammunition (anonymized proof posts,
outreach personalization). The product manufactures its own content.

## 4. Marketing plan (the awareness gap, filled cheaply)

1. **Proof-of-life content (now, $0):** one anonymized hit per week on
   LinkedIn ("flagged this CFO search six weeks before the posting —
   here were the signs"), authored from real briefs, posted by Rich.
   This is the only content that is simultaneously credible, unfakeable,
   and produced as a by-product of operations.
2. **Referral engine (now, 20% of revenue):** formalize the Alexei deal
   as a standard: 20% first-year commission, coach-channel and
   peer-recruiter referrals. Add the referral ask to pilot onboarding
   and every positive feedback moment (playbook R10).
3. **GEO/SEO (month 2-3, ~$500-1,000/mo experiment):** the ChatGPT/Google
   discovery question Curtis couldn't answer. Start with the free layer:
   a "how executive searches really start" evidence page per niche on
   mandatesignal.com (the site rewrite's /learn-more diligence pattern,
   pointed outward), schema markup, and the weekly posts republished as
   indexed articles. Measure branded search + AI-referral traffic before
   spending more. A niche freelancer only after the free layer shows pull.
4. **The inbound form** stays the L2 lane with metered contacts —
   already correct; content above feeds it.
5. **NOT yet:** PR, paid social, sponsorships, SEO agencies. Revisit at
   first 10 paying customers when CAC math exists.

## 5. Pre-registered metrics (decided before the data arrives)

| Metric | 30 days | 90 days |
|---|---|---|
| Paying MandateSignal customers ($250 pilot counts) | 3 | 10 |
| Sample→per-mandate subscription conversion | first 1 | ≥40% of samples |
| True-cold qualified conversations (Mo tracks) | ≥8 | ≥30 |
| DMQ sends (trigger-gated, from queue) | ≥40 | ≥150 |
| N1 vs old-opener 10v10 verdict | recorded | acted on |
| Weekly proof posts published | 4 | 12 |
| Referral-sourced conversations | 2 | 8 |
| Free-lead firms converted (Michael/Todd/Sylvie cohort) | decision each | — |

Kill rules: any channel below half its 90-day target with ≥80% of its
budget spent gets rebuilt or cut — volume is never the response to
underperformance (standing rule). The free-lead cohort is the
highest-information experiment in the company: every firm currently
holding free leads gets an explicit convert-or-close conversation within
30 days. "Still checking them out" past day 30 is a no; log it as one.
