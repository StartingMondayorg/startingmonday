# The Business of Starting Monday and MandateSignal

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Status:** Internal reference — not for external distribution
**Sources:** Sales & Marketing Plan v1.1 (2026-08-03), Signal Engine Cross-Product Master Plan v1.1 (2026-07-26), product requirements, brand configuration

## 1. One Business, Two Products, One Engine

Starting Monday and MandateSignal are two products built on one signal engine, sold to the two sides of the same market event: an executive leadership transition.

- **Starting Monday** (startingmonday.app) serves the **candidate side** — executives running a search, and the coaches who advise them. It is a relationship-building operating system with an intelligence layer: watch chosen companies, detect precursor signals and leadership roles, and convert them into specific conversations, follow-ups, and pipeline actions.
- **MandateSignal** (mandatesignal.com) serves the **search side** — boutique and transition-focused executive search firms. It delivers mandate-level intelligence: evidence that a leadership search is opening at a specific company, before the posting, packaged as a lead the recruiter can act on. The lead *is* the message.

The products share a signal-engine logical domain but are deliberately **not merged**: separate databases, deployments, customer data, release processes, and repositories. They exchange only approved aggregate learning artifacts, and prediction or performance claims are published only after calibration gates pass. Brand resolution is host-based — the same platform code renders as MandateSignal on its domain and Starting Monday everywhere else.

## 2. Why the Same Event Sells Twice

A single verified precursor — an activist filing, a CFO's insider sales, a board change, a funding round followed by an ATS posting spike — is valuable to:

1. **The executive** who wants to be in relationship with that company before the role is public (Starting Monday).
2. **The search firm** that wants to win the mandate to fill it (MandateSignal).

Neither buyer competes with the other, the underlying collection and verification cost is paid once, and each product's usage produces labels that make the shared engine better. That is the structural advantage: one intelligence cost base, two non-conflicting revenue lines, compounding data.

## 3. MandateSignal — The Revenue Priority

**Motion:** founder-led, lead-led, low-volume precision. Trigger-gated messages built on a real, provable lead; Rich sends and closes. Agencies and SDRs are structurally wrong for this motion — they cannot carry founder credibility, hold a research-frame conversation with a search professional, or fake possession of a real lead.

**Pricing ladder (ratified 2026-08-03):** $250 pilot, converting to a per-mandate subscription at **$1,000/mandate/month**, with the pilot fee credited on conversion.

**Target model:** ~100 customers at $1,000/mandate/month = **$100K MRR**. The addressable pool is a few hundred boutiques per niche, and niches do not refill when burned — which is why generic sequences to recruiters are prohibited and recruiter lists are frozen out of volume tooling.

**Proof asset:** the brief. Every MandateSignal brief is simultaneously delivery and marketing ammunition — anonymized proof posts ("flagged this CFO search six weeks before the posting — here were the signs") are the only content that is credible, unfakeable, and produced as a by-product of operations.

**Pre-registered 90-day metrics:** 10 paying customers ($250 pilots count), ≥40% sample-to-subscription conversion, ≥150 trigger-gated sends, 12 proof posts. Kill rule: any channel below half its 90-day target with 80% of budget spent gets rebuilt or cut; volume is never the response to underperformance.

## 4. Starting Monday — The Channel and Volume Product

**Motion:** product-led and outsourced-mechanical. Candidates and coaches are numerous; conversations are optional. Sequences, volume email, and RevOps automation belong here: coach-channel nurture, signal-triggered executive outreach using the product's own briefing data, and executives-in-transition lists. The product-led trial does the closing where possible.

**Pricing structure:** three public tiers — **Monitor / Active / Executive**. The trial grants active-tier behavior, and the first briefing arrives the next day. Standard scan cadence is three scans per week; the Executive tier adds daily plus evening scans.

**Buyers and value:**

- **Executives:** earlier timing, better focus, higher-quality action, lower cognitive load, confidentiality for a quiet search.
- **Coaches and search advisors:** leverage (monitoring becomes part of the client operating system), better conversations grounded in real signals, retention beyond a single search, and a shared evidence record.

**Relationship layer as the moat-facing product:** contacts, People to Reach, the Outreach Hub, follow-up manager and calendar, pipeline context, briefing rhythm, and board-track cadence. The scanner tells the user *when* and *why now*; the relationship layer is *what they do about it* — and where retention lives after the search ends.

**Channel economics:** referral engine formalized at 20% first-year commission (the Alexei standard), coach-channel nurture under Mo's RevOps machine ($2K/month with pre-registered month-3 performance gates), GEO/SEO experiment at $500–1,000/month from month two. Total operating budget held under $4K/month until 10 paying customers establish real CAC.

## 5. The Flywheel

1. Signals are collected once, across 19 active streams (SEC family, WARN notices, ATS feeds, press, licensed vendors, career pages).
2. Starting Monday converts them into briefings, outreach, and pipeline actions for candidates; MandateSignal converts them into mandate leads and briefs for search firms.
3. Every brief and briefing is marketing ammunition (anonymized proof posts, outreach personalization). The product manufactures its own content.
4. Every user action, verified hit, verified miss, and outcome label improves precision, recall, lead-time evidence, and calibration.
5. Better calibration means stronger, provable claims — which close the next pilot.

**The moat is the label store.** Anyone can fetch today's news. The defensible asset is the historical dataset connecting precursor events, leadership changes, job openings, role families, and time-to-opening — accumulated per-customer and per-niche, funded by every close.

## 6. Operating Discipline

- **Honest baseline first:** revenue targets, gates, and kill rules are pre-registered before results arrive, so outcomes are measurements, not moods.
- **Evidence states govern claims:** every capability is tracked as CLAIMED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → DEPLOYED → MEASURED (or BLOCKED_EXTERNAL). Repository inspection alone never establishes deployed or measured status.
- **Compliance is a launch control:** the source catalog carries per-source rights metadata; a fail-closed registry blocks any fetcher that is not explicitly allowed; vendor restrictions (GNews paid tier, PDL aggregation limits, Apollo publication approval) are tracked with owners and deadlines.
- **Niche protection:** recruiter niches are finite and non-renewing; MandateSignal prospecting stays trigger-gated and founder-sent, isolated from volume sequences.
- **Founder time is the scarce resource:** ≤12 hours/week selling, protected; everything mechanical is automated or outsourced when it earns its cost.

## 7. Risk Posture

| Risk | Acceptance rationale / mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Rich is the closing bottleneck | By design at this stage; founder-led sales is where the learning lives, and every close funds the label store |
| Candidate-side revenue stays secondary this quarter | Coach channel and pilots keep it warm without stealing MandateSignal focus |
| Single-channel concentration (LinkedIn + founder account) | Mitigated by email recovery, content, and referrals — not eliminated |
| Source fragility (career-page redesigns, anti-bot, vendor terms) | Layered fetching, explicit failure states, compliance metadata, manual-review fallback |
| Claim risk (overpromising prediction) | Calibration gates before any published performance claim; conservative public contract for the scanner |
