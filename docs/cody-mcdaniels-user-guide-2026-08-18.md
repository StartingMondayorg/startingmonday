## What this product is for

Starting Monday is a relationship-building workbench. It helps you decide:

1. Which companies are worth pursuing.
2. Which people are worth knowing before a role opens.
3. What your angle is with each person.
4. What to say next.
5. Which conversations and follow-ups need attention today.

It does not replace your judgment or send messages for you. You choose the people, verify the context, and send the outreach yourself.

## Your next-action loop

### Daily, 20 minutes

1. Open **Today**, not the full dashboard.
2. Pick one target company.
3. Pick one target person and one backup person.
4. Read the company signal and the person's role.
5. Write one reason this person would care about your experience.
6. Check whether a warm connection exists.
7. Send or schedule one human-reviewed LinkedIn note.
8. Record the action and the next follow-up date.

### Weekly, 45 minutes

- Keep five to eight active companies.
- Keep two to four people per active company.
- Remove companies where the role lane, timing, or geography no longer fits.
- Review replies and choose the next action for every live conversation.
- Generate prep only for meetings or conversations that are actually scheduled.

## How to use the current dashboard

- **Add a company:** use this for a company you would genuinely consider joining.
- **Company signal / brief:** use this to understand why the company may be relevant now.
- **Contacts:** use this to record the people you are intentionally building relationships with.
- **Get brief:** use this before a real meeting, first interview, or important outreach. It should help you prepare; it is not a task list by itself.
- **Feedback:** mark what was useful or wrong so the system can improve its recommendations.
- **Pipeline:** update the relationship state after a real action or response.

## What not to do

- Do not start with every company in the database.
- Do not contact three executives at one company with the same message on the same day.
- Do not claim that a signal proves a job is opening.
- Do not use a generic resume dump as the first message.
- Do not generate a meeting brief before there is a meeting.
- Do not treat an inferred penalty, score, or internal label as user-facing truth.

## A simpler mental model

Starting Monday should answer one question at a time:

> Who should I get to know next, why now, and what is the smallest useful action I can take today?
